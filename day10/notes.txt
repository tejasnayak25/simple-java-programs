Sorting techniques:
    Sorting is a process of arranging all elements in ordered manner.
    Ascending / Descending

Types of sorting techniques:
1) Selection sort
2) Insertion sort
3) Bubble sort
4) Merge sort
5) Quick sort
6) Radix sort
7) Heap sort

Selection sort:
    is the process of select the each and every element from the unsorted array, compare and swap repeatedly to make a sorted array.

Ex:
40 30 10 20

Step 1:
i = 0, j = 1
    40 > 30 - True

    30 40 10 20

i = 0, j = 2
    30 > 10 - True

    10 40 30 20

i = 0, j = 3
    10 > 20 - False

Step 2:
i = 1, j = 2
    40 > 30 - True

    10 30 40 20

i = 1, j = 3
    30 > 20 - True

    10 20 40 30

Step 3:
i = 2, j = 3
    40 > 30 - True

    10 20 30 40

Insertion sort:
    is the process of inserting the each and every element from unsorted subarray to sorted subarray.

Step 1:
Assume 0th index is a sorted subarray, rest of all is unsorted subarray.

Step 2:
Assume 1st index is a temp.

Step 3:
If temp value is less than previous value, swap, else temp = temp.next

Step 4:
Repeat step 3 until sorted array is found.

BubbleSort:
    only compares adjacent elements.

Rule of BubbleSort:
Placing largest number at the correct position.
